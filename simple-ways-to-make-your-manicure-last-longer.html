Simple Ways to Make Your Manicure Last Longer

A little care before and after your appointment can help keep your manicure looking neat for longer. These simple habits work for both salon and at-home manicures.

Prepare your nails gently

Start with clean, dry nails. Remove any old polish and wipe away residue before applying a new colour, as oils or leftover product can affect how evenly polish sits. Avoid soaking your hands just before a manicure: nails can absorb water and change shape slightly as they dry.

Push back cuticles gently rather than cutting them yourself. If you have a sore, damaged or inflamed area around a nail, let it settle and ask a nail professional for advice before having a manicure.

Apply polish in thin layers

Thick coats can take longer to dry and may smudge more easily. Apply a base coat, then build colour with thin, even layers, allowing each one to dry before adding the next. Finish with a top coat, and brush a little over the free edge of each nail to help protect the tips.

Give your manicure enough time to set before reaching into a bag, putting on shoes or doing chores. Polish may feel dry on the surface before it has hardened fully.

Protect your hands during everyday tasks

Wear gloves when washing dishes, cleaning or working with products that could dry out your skin or affect the polish. Try not to use your nails to open packaging, scrape labels or press hard buttons; use your fingertips or a suitable tool instead.

Frequent contact with water and household cleaners can be tough on both polish and the skin around your nails. After washing your hands, dry them well and apply hand cream.

Keep nails and cuticles moisturised

A small amount of cuticle oil or hand cream can help keep the surrounding skin comfortable and tidy. Apply it regularly, especially after washing your hands. Avoid picking at dry skin or chipped polish, as this can damage the nail surface.

Refresh carefully

A thin layer of top coat can help revive shine and protect a manicure as it wears. If a small chip appears, smooth the edge gently and touch it up with matching polish rather than peeling the colour away. Peeling can remove layers of the natural nail, so use the appropriate remover when it is time to take polish off.

For gel or other long-wear manicures, follow your nail technician’s removal advice. If you are unsure which products or aftercare suit your nails, ask your salon professional at Purely Lovely for guidance.
