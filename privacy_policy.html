Purely Lovely Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Purely Lovely (“we”, “us” or “our”) is a beauty salon in England. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you visit our salon, make or manage an appointment, contact us, or use our website or social media pages.

  1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, contact details, appointment and service history, payment and transaction information, and messages or feedback you send us. We may also collect information relevant to providing a service safely, such as allergies, sensitivities, skin or nail conditions, and other health information you choose to share. If you use our website, limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited may be collected through cookies or similar technologies.

Please do not provide health information unless it is relevant to your treatment or safety.

  1. How and why we use your information

We use personal information to manage bookings, provide and personalise salon services, communicate with you, process payments, respond to enquiries, maintain business records, protect our staff and customers, and meet legal obligations. We may also use information to improve our services and, where permitted, to send you marketing communications. You can opt out of marketing at any time.

  1. Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of these lawful bases: performing a contract with you, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing our legitimate interests where those interests are not overridden by your rights, or your consent. Where we process health information, we will do so only where a lawful condition for processing special category data applies, such as your explicit consent or where processing is necessary to provide safe treatment and is permitted by law. You may withdraw consent, but this may mean we cannot provide a particular service safely.

  1. Sharing your information

We may share information with service providers who help us run our business, such as booking, payment, website hosting or communications providers, and with professional advisers or public authorities where required by law. We require service providers to protect information and use it only for authorised purposes. We do not sell your personal information.

  1. International transfers

If a service provider processes your information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by data protection law to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place.

  1. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain appropriate business and financial records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason we hold it. When information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

  1. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to make it work, understand how it is used, or support other features. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any cookie controls provided on our website.

  1. Keeping your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

  1. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct it, ask for it to be erased or restricted, object to certain processing, receive certain information in a portable format, or withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

  1. Children

Our services are intended for adults unless a particular service is expressly offered to a younger person with any required parent or guardian involvement. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information unnecessarily.

  1. Contact us

For questions about this policy or to exercise your data protection rights, contact Purely Lovely using the contact details provided in your booking confirmation or through the contact channel you normally use to reach the salon.

  1. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our usual customer communication channels or on our website, if applicable.
